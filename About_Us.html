About WildHeart Eco Park

Where wildlife, education and conservation come together.

Our Story

WildHeart Eco Park is a wildlife attraction designed to connect visitors with nature through immersive habitats, educational experiences and conservation-focused activities. Our park celebrates the beauty of the natural world while encouraging visitors to understand why protecting wildlife matters.

Our Mission

🌿 Protect

Support wildlife conservation and raise awareness about endangered species and fragile habitats.

📚 Educate

Help visitors learn about animals, ecosystems and the importance of sustainable living.

🦁 Inspire

Create memorable experiences that inspire people to care for the planet and its wildlife.
