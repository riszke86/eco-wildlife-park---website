Canopy of the Amazons

Discover one of the world's richest rainforests, home to colourful birds, rescued sloths and tropical wildlife.

About This Habitat

Canopy of the Amazons is a tropical rainforest habitat inspired by the rich biodiversity of the Amazon. Visitors can discover colourful birds, slow-moving sloths, tree frogs, tropical plants and learn why protecting rainforest ecosystems is essential.
